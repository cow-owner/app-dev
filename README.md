# Favorite Series: Severance

## Overview
Severance is a psychological sci-fi thriller series about employees at *Lumon Industries* whose memories are surgically divided between their work and personal lives.

## Why It Hits Different
* **Pacing:** Slow-burn tension that actually pays off.
* **Cinematography:** Symmetrical framing and liminal office aesthetics.
* **Writing:** Zero hand-holding; respects the audience's intelligence.
